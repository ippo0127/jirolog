# About Jiro Log

**Jiro Log** (二郎ログ) is an unofficial fan site about the official Ramen Jiro shops. It is not affiliated with Ramen Jiro or any of its shops.

## What you can do here

- See hours, closing days, access and house rules for every official shop
- Find what is open right now, in Japan time (public holidays included)
- Browse the map or sort shops by distance from you
- Follow the [lineage](lineage.html): which shop each owner trained at
- Keep your own visit log and work toward visiting every shop ([My Jiro log](log.html))

## Sources

- [Ramen Scene](https://ramen-scene.com) (ラーメンシーン): a Japanese blog covering every Jiro shop. Shop pages link to its articles.
- Chokkei Jiro Daisuki Man (直系二郎大好きマン — [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/delicious26261126/) · [X](https://x.com/delicious2626) · [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCnblzHDvdCnrvx2hgaq8bpw)): a Japanese creator who eats at Jiro almost every day. Shop pages link to their videos and posts.
- Each shop’s official X account or website, Ramen Database, Tabelog, and shop-info roundup sites. Every shop page lists the sources used and when.

Both are in Japanese. Links marked **JA** go to Japanese pages.

## Please note

- Hours are a guide. Shops close early when the noodles run out, and they sometimes close for the day. Check the official account before you go.
- Prices show when they were last checked.
- The Japanese pages are the main version. Where an English translation is missing, you will see the Japanese text.
